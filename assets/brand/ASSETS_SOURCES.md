## Usage Guidelines

### DO's
- ✅ Use DOG gold color (#f4b942) for accents
- ✅ Mention "Built for the DOG Army"
- ✅ Use "Bitcoin L1 native" descriptor
- ✅ Show Kraken CLI attribution
- ✅ Keep paper-only messaging clear

### DON'Ts
- ❌ Claim official DOG Runes affiliation
- ❌ Claim Kraken partnership
- ❌ Use Dogecoin (XDGUSD) imagery
- ❌ Show live trading buttons
- ❌ Hide paper-only nature
